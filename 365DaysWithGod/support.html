App Support

Thank you for your interest in A Closer Walk: 365 Days With God. The app is currently in development.

What will the app include?

365 Scripture-centered devotionals, private journaling, a prayer-request history, favorites, and open access to every daily reading.

Will I need internet access?

The app is being developed for offline use after installation.

Can I read ahead?

Yes. All 365 devotionals are planned to be available from the start.

Where will my journal and prayer entries be saved?

The app is designed to store them locally on your device. Please review the Privacy Policy.

How can I get additional help?

Visit A Closer Walk for ministry information and contact options. A dedicated app-support contact method can be added here before launch.
