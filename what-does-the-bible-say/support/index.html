Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

The app's Bible text, curated study content, Scripture search, and verse lookup are designed to work offline. If a particular feature does not work as expected, please contact support.

Which Bible translations are available?

The app provides World English Bible (WEB) and King James Version (KJV) translations. Look for the translation selector in the app.

What if I find an incorrect Bible reference or answer?

Please email the topic or question, the Scripture reference, and a brief description so we can review the content.

What if a page is blank or the Back button behaves unexpectedly?

Close and reopen the app, then try the same steps again. If the issue continues, email the section name and navigation steps. Avoid deleting the app before contacting support.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Visit the What Does the Bible Say? Privacy Policy.
