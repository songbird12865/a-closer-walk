What Does the Bible Say?
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

A Closer Walk Ministries respects your privacy. This policy describes how the What Does the Bible Say? iPhone and iPad application is designed to handle information.

1. Offline Bible study

The app is designed to provide its Bible text, Scripture search, verse lookup, and curated study material directly on your device. These features are intended to work without an account or a connection to an app-operated server.

2. Information collected by the app

The app is designed not to request your name, email address, location, or account registration to use its Bible-study features. It does not include advertising or AI-powered questions sent to an external service. The app is not intended to collect or transmit your Bible searches, verse lookups, or study questions to A Closer Walk Ministries.

Apple may separately process information associated with downloading, purchasing, or using apps through its own services, subject to Apple's policies.

3. Support communications

If you choose to email us for assistance, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, device details, and description of the issue. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information.

4. Website links

The app or its listing may link to A Closer Walk Ministries' website and support pages. Visiting a website requires an internet connection and is separate from the app's offline Bible-study features. The website and your browser or hosting provider may process technical information associated with a web visit.

5. Children's privacy

The app is designed for general audiences and does not require children to provide personal information to use its study features. Parents or guardians should contact us on a child's behalf for support.

6. Security and updates

We aim to minimize personal information handling by keeping Bible-study functionality on the device. We may update this policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. The current version will be posted at this address.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact Mel@acloserwalk.org.
