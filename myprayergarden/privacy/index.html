Privacy Policy

✦ · ✦

Your prayers, reflections, and testimonies are deeply personal. This page explains the app’s intended approach to privacy.

Before public release

This policy describes intended practices for My Prayer Garden, which is still in development. It must be reviewed against the final iOS build and updated before public release.

Personalization without an account

The app is designed to let you choose a profile name that personalizes your Prayer Garden, Prayer Journal, and Testimony Journal. No email address or account registration is planned.

Prayers and testimonies

Your prayer requests, prayer histories, journal reflections, answered prayers, and blessings are intended to be stored locally on your device. They are not intended to be transmitted to A Closer Walk Ministries or to an AI service.

Advertising and tracking

No third-party advertising, analytics tracking, or behavioral profiling is planned for the app.

Device backups and export

Locally stored content may be included in device backups depending on your device settings. Deleting the app may remove locally stored content. Optional secure backup and export features are planned; their exact behavior will be documented when implemented.

Optional device permissions

Any optional features requiring device permissions, such as notifications or photo access, will be described and requested only when needed. These features are not required for the basic prayer journal.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store purchases and certain diagnostics under its own privacy policies. These processes are separate from the app's locally stored prayer content.

Changes to this policy

We will review and update this page if the application's features or information-handling practices change.

Questions or concerns

Contact mel@acloserwalk.org or visit our Support page.
