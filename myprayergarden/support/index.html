Frequently asked questions

Growing together in prayer

How does the Living Prayer Garden work?

Each prayer request becomes a flower. As you return to pray, its appearance changes through growth, blooming, and color changes. These changes represent continued prayer, not a prediction of God's response.

How do I pray over an existing request?

Open its flower and select Pray Again. You can record a new prayer or reflection and revisit its history.

What happens when a prayer is answered?

When you personally mark a prayer answered, its flower becomes a Golden Bloom and the prayer is included in the Answered Prayers section of your Testimony Journal. Its original history remains connected.

Can I record blessings that weren't prayer requests?

Yes. The planned Things God Has Done for You section lets you record blessings, gratitude, and testimonies independently of existing prayer requests.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is planned, and core prayer and journaling features are designed to work offline.

Where will my entries be saved?

Profile names, prayers, and testimonies are intended to remain on your device. Please review our Privacy Policy. Preserve backups of important entries when the app supports export.

How can I get help?

Email mel@acloserwalk.org for assistance or feedback.
