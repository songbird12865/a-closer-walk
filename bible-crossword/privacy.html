How Bible Crossword handles information and respects your privacy.

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

A Closer Walk Ministries respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Bible Crossword: A Closer Walk handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Bible Crossword does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information through the app.

The app does not require users to create an online account, provide an email address, or submit a real name.

Players may create in-game player profiles. These profiles, along with game progress, puzzle completion, badges, awards, and related game information, are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to provide the game's features.

Children's Privacy

Bible Crossword is designed to be suitable for a broad audience, including families. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Players are not required to provide their real names or other personally identifying information to use the app.

Internet and External Services

The core Bible Crossword game and its content are bundled with the application and do not require access to a hosted game website to play.

The app does not use advertising networks, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics to track users.

Photos and Saved Awards

If a player chooses to save a custom award, certificate, or other game-created image to the device's photo library, the app may request permission from the operating system to save that image. This permission is used only for the requested save function.

The app does not upload or otherwise collect the user's photos.

Game Content and Local Data

Information such as player profiles, puzzle progress, completed difficulty levels, badges, awards, and other game preferences may be stored locally on the device so that players can continue their progress.

A Closer Walk Ministries does not receive this locally stored information through the app.

Data Sharing

Because Bible Crossword does not collect personal information through the app, A Closer Walk Ministries does not sell, rent, or share app-user personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted with a revised effective date.
