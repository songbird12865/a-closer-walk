Privacy Policy

Status: Pre-release policy draft. This page describes the intended app design and must be checked against the final released build before publication.

Your devotional experience

365 Days With God is being designed as an offline devotional app. Its devotional readings are included within the app.

Journal and prayer entries

The app is designed to save personal journal entries and prayer requests locally on your device. No account or sign-in is planned. Entries are not intended to be sent to A Closer Walk Ministries or to an AI service.

Device storage and backups

Entries may be included in device backups according to your device's backup settings. Deleting the app may remove locally stored entries. Any future export or backup feature will be described here before release.

Third-party services

No advertising, analytics, or remote AI processing is currently planned within the app. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies.

Updates

This policy will be reviewed and updated before App Store release if the implementation or data practices change.

Questions

For support information, visit our Support page or A Closer Walk website.
