Privacy Policy

✦ · ✦

Your personal reflections and prayers deserve thoughtful care. Here is how this devotional app is designed to handle your information.

Privacy and your personal entries

This policy describes the intended privacy practices of the combined devotional and prayer journaling app. Confirm these statements against the final release build, including any export, restore, or diagnostic features, before publishing.

What the app includes

365 Days With God combines Scripture-centered daily devotionals, guided reflections, a personal reflection journal, and My Prayer Journal with prayer gardens, prayer entries, answered-prayer records, and testimony journaling.

Information you enter

Your reflection entries, prayer titles and text, chosen flowers and gardens, prayer status, answered-prayer details, and testimony entries are intended to be saved locally on your device. No account or sign-in is planned.

Offline access and data sharing

The devotional and prayer experience is designed for offline use. Personal journal, prayer, and testimony content is not intended to be transmitted to A Closer Walk Ministries or sent to an AI service.

Device storage and backups

Locally saved content may be included in device backups depending on your device settings. Deleting the app may delete locally stored reflections, gardens, prayers, and testimonies. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step without considering your data.

Purchases and third parties

365 Days With God is offered for a one-time price of $5.99 through the App Store. Apple processes purchases and may collect diagnostics according to its own policies. The app is not designed to display advertisements or use remote AI processing.

Children and personal information

The app does not require users to create an account. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information, including private prayer or journal entries, in support emails.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. Any material changes should be reflected here.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email mel@acloserwalk.org or visit the Support page.
