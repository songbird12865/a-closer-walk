Privacy Policy

✦ · ✦

Your personal reflections and prayers deserve thoughtful care. Here is how this devotional app is designed to handle your information.

Before public release

This policy describes the app’s intended data practices. Its statements should be checked against the final iOS build before the app is released.

Your devotional experience

365 Days With God is designed as an offline devotional app, with Scripture readings and devotional content included in the app.

Journal and prayer entries

Personal journal entries and prayer requests are designed to be stored locally on your device. No account or sign-in is planned. Entries are not intended to be sent to A Closer Walk Ministries or to an AI service.

Device storage and backups

Entries may be included in device backups according to your device settings. Deleting the app may remove locally stored entries. Any future export or backup feature will be described here before release.

Third-party services

No advertising, analytics, or remote AI processing is currently planned within the app. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be reviewed and updated before App Store release if the implementation or data practices change.

Questions or privacy concerns

Contact mel@acloserwalk.org or visit our Support page.
