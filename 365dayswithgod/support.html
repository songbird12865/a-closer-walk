Frequently asked questions

Everything you need for your walk

What does the app include?

The planned experience includes 365 Scripture-centered devotionals, personal journaling, a prayer-request history, favorites, and the freedom to explore daily readings.

Do I need an internet connection?

The devotional experience is designed for offline use after the app has been installed.

Can I read ahead?

Yes. The app is designed to let you explore any of the 365 devotionals at your own pace.

Where are my journal and prayer entries stored?

The app is designed to keep your personal entries locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

How can I contact support?

For questions, assistance, or feedback, email mel@acloserwalk.org. You can also visit the A Closer Walk website.

Our purpose

Every devotional is an invitation to slow down, open Scripture, and take another step in your walk with God.
