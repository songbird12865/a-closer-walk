Frequently asked questions

Devotionals and prayer gardens in one app

What is included with my $5.99 purchase?

A one-time purchase includes all 365 dated devotionals and the integrated My Prayer Journal. Explore daily Scripture, reflections, favorites, personal journaling, seven prayer gardens, prayer entries, and testimony records.

Can I read ahead?

Yes. Browse any of the 365 devotionals at any time. The devotional year is organized around 2027.

How do I use My Prayer Journal?

Open My Prayer Journal to write and save prayers, select a flower and garden, plant your prayer, nurture it through continued prayer, and mark it answered when appropriate.

What happens when my gardens fill up?

Seven gardens are available initially, with 12 planting spaces each. Another set of seven gardens unlocks when the existing set is full.

Can I record answered prayers?

Yes. Mark prayers answered and use the Testimony Journal to revisit and record what God has done.

What daily prayer content is included?

My Prayer Journal includes Pause with Scripture and Quiet Moment with God daily readings. The app supports WEB and KJV Scripture options.

Will my entries be private?

The app is designed to store personal reflections, prayers, and testimony entries on your device. Please read the Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app need internet?

The devotional and prayer journaling content is designed for offline use once installed. Downloading, purchasing, or updating the app requires access to the App Store.

How can I get help?

Email mel@acloserwalk.org with your device, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please do not include private prayer entries unless you choose to share them.
